## Style Prompt

Proof Ledger Noir. A dark, premium, judge-facing motion language for AI x Web3: less cyberpunk, more credible product theater. The video should feel like a polished launch reel for a real startup product, using restrained movement, editorial headlines, soft proof glows, and evidence-heavy panels. Motion should communicate clarity and trust instead of hype noise.

## Colors

- `#0b0f0d` background
- `#f4efe2` primary text
- `#8ef5c6` mint proof accent
- `#f5b36b` warm spotlight accent
- `#1b2320` panel surface

## Typography

- `Newsreader` for display headlines
- `Manrope` for interface text, labels, and proof data

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use purple or blue neon gradients.
- Do not use generic dashboard card grids as the main visual.
- Do not use playful bounce motion or elastic scene transitions.
- Do not center every composition; keep an editorial left-led rhythm.
- Do not make it look like a speculative crypto meme trailer.
